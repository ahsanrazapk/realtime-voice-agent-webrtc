My own audio pipeline from the phone to the AI and back, built without third-party call platforms.

  • Flutter app streams the microphone over WebRTC to a FastAPI + aiortc server
  • Voice activity detection decides when you have finished speaking
  • Groq runs Whisper, Llama 3.3 70B and Orpheus for a fast spoken reply
